Unit 2: Grammar 2 (Full Practice)

GRAMMAR REFERENCE: Simple Present vs. Present Continuous
Use Case Simple Present Present Continuous
Facts / Now My company employs 250 people. My company is recruiting more staff.
Routines / Temporary I start work at nine. This week, I'm starting early at eight.
Regular Change / Situation The cost of tickets goes up every year. Public transportation is getting more expensive.
"Every time" / "All the time" He always arrives on time. He's always complaining! (Annoyance)
Stative Verbs (State/Opinion): use simple present. (know, like, love, believe, understand).
Dynamic Verbs (Action): use both. (work, develop, meet).
A. Make sentences using the correct tense.
1. We / currently / develop / a new product.
2. Once a day, / my department / meet / for ten minutes.
3. Sorry, I / not / understand / the question.
4. At the moment, / I / feel / great about my promotion.
5. The management / always / change / how we work.
6. He / speak / to someone. Can he call you back?
B. Choose the correct interpretation.
1. "Eduardo is coming from Colombia."
2. "He's being helpful today."
3. "I have a meeting at 3."
4. "I'm thinking about it."
5. "He's very helpful."
C. Complaints: Order the words (Always + Continuous).
1. (always / they / complaining / are / about / work)
2. (is / he / talking / always / phone / on / the)
3. (forgetting / always / she / is / her / keys)
4. (are / you / always / arriving / late / class / to)
