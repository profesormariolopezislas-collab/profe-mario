Grammar Reference: Simple Present vs. Present Continuous
Use Case Simple Present Present Continuous
General facts / Now My company employs 250 people. My company is recruiting more staff.
Stative Verbs I know what you mean. (Believe) (Not usually used in continuous)
Annoyance - We're always having meetings!
A. Make sentences using the correct tense.
1. We / currently / develop / a new product.
2. Once a day, / my department / meet / for ten minutes.
3. Sorry, I / not / understand / the question.
B. Choose the correct interpretation for each sentence.
1. "Eduardo is coming from Colombia."
2. "He's being helpful today."
C. Complaints: Order the words correctly (Always + Continuous).
1. (always / they / complaining / are / about / work)
2. (is / he / talking / always / phone / on / the)
